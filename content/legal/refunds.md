# MÚRÀ Refund Policy
Last Updated: September 2026

MÚRÀ sells digital products.

Because digital products can be delivered and accessed immediately, MÚRÀ generally does not accept change-of-mind refunds after a digital product has been successfully delivered or downloaded.

However, MÚRÀ will consider legitimate refund and remedy requests in the circumstances described below and will comply with applicable consumer-protection law.

## 1. Digital Products

Before purchasing, customers should review the product description, included components, previews, and price carefully.

Once a digital product has been successfully delivered or made available for download, a refund will generally not be provided solely because:

- You changed your mind.
- You no longer need the product.
- You purchased the product accidentally.
- You expected the product to contain something that was not included in its product description.
- You did not review the product description before purchasing.
- You purchased a product for someone else and they do not want it.

This policy does not remove any consumer rights that cannot legally be excluded.

## 2. Duplicate Purchases

If you accidentally purchase the same MÚRÀ product more than once, contact us as soon as possible.

Where we can verify that the purchases are genuine duplicate transactions for the same customer and product, MÚRÀ may provide an appropriate remedy, which may include a refund of the duplicate purchase where appropriate.

## 3. Incorrect Product Delivered

If MÚRÀ provides you with a product different from the product you purchased, contact us.

After verification, MÚRÀ may provide the correct product or another appropriate remedy.

## 4. Corrupted or Defective Files

If a purchased file is corrupted, inaccessible, materially defective, or cannot reasonably be used because of a technical problem attributable to MÚRÀ, contact us.

We will first attempt to resolve the issue by providing a working replacement file or another appropriate solution.

If the issue cannot reasonably be resolved, an appropriate refund or other remedy may be provided where applicable.

## 6. Unauthorized Transactions

If you believe a transaction was made without your authorization, contact MÚRÀ and, where applicable, the payment provider immediately.

MÚRÀ may investigate the transaction and may request reasonable information necessary to verify the claim.

Nothing in this section limits rights or remedies available under applicable law or through the relevant payment provider.

## 7. Fraudulent or Abusive Refund Requests

MÚRÀ may investigate refund requests where there is evidence of fraud, deception, abuse, repeated misuse of the refund process, or other unlawful activity.

MÚRÀ may refuse a refund request where the request is inconsistent with the facts of the transaction or applicable law.

MÚRÀ may also restrict access or take other appropriate action where there is evidence of abuse or unlawful activity.

## 8. Downloaded Products

A digital product that has already been successfully delivered or downloaded is generally not eligible for a change-of-mind refund.

Customers should review product descriptions and previews before purchasing.

## 9. Refund Processing

Where a refund is approved, it will generally be processed through the original payment method where technically and legally possible.

The time required for the funds to appear in your account may depend on the payment provider, bank, card issuer, or other financial institution involved.

MÚRÀ does not control the processing time of external financial institutions.

## 11. Consumer Rights

This Refund Policy does not attempt to exclude, restrict, or waive any consumer right that cannot legally be excluded, restricted, or waived.

Where applicable law gives a customer a mandatory right to a refund, replacement, cancellation, return, or other remedy, that right will apply regardless of this Policy.
